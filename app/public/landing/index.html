Recall

Keep the skills you worked for.

Skills are like muscles: they weaken without training. Recall keeps the data skills your next job asks for, in five minutes a day.

How it works

1. Pick the jobs you want and the topics you studied. No transcript needed.
2. Recall ranks your skills by how often real job postings ask for them and how likely they are to be fading.
3. Each day you answer three short questions and, some days, one work scenario. Each answer comes with a short explanation.
